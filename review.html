About Review data

Review activity stays in this browser. It records app visits, approximate active time, and whether an app saved a change. It does not copy the contents of your dashboard records into the activity log. Apps hosted on the separate private Atlas origin can currently be counted when launched from Atlas, but their inside-app activity is not visible to this browser-local Review.
