Connect iCloud

Use your Apple Account email and an app-specific password. Your main Apple password is not needed.

Create a password at Apple ↗ → Sign-In and Security → App-Specific Passwords. Name it Life Map.

How your connection is protected

The connection is saved encrypted in your private Atlas workspace. Your password is never sent to Life Map or included in backups. Only calendars you select will be read. You can disconnect here and revoke the app-specific password at Apple.
