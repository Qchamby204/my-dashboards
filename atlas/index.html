Bring in earlier sessions

In the original Master Communicator, choose Export progress, then select that JSON file here.

Imports completed session dates, topics, drills, and skills. Assessments, grades, transcripts, and preparation notes stay in the original app. Dates use this device’s time zone when first imported.
